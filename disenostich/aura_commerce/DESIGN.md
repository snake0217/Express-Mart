## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-density, high-conversion global commerce engine engineered for extreme clarity, rapid scanability, and transactional trust. It bridges the authoritative scale of legacy global marketplaces with the refined precision of contemporary digital product design. 

The aesthetic is a hybrid of Modern Corporate utility and Tactile Micro-Interactions:
- **Tone & Persona:** Utilitarian, exceptionally dependable, vibrant yet structured, frictionless.
- **Visual Stance:** Eliminates visual clutter through strict structural grids, precise typographic rhythm, and deliberate chromatic cues that immediately signal purchase intent, trust verifications, and urgent time-sensitive offers.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute buyer confidence, effortless navigational orientation across millions of SKUs, and immediate visual reward during conversion funnels.

## Colors

The palette leverages a strategic hierarchy calibrated for conversion velocity, high-contrast readability, and immediate navigational hierarchy:

- **Primary Accent (`#ea580c` - Energetic Amber-Orange):** Reserved exclusively for high-intent conversion actions—such as primary checkout buttons, "Buy Now" triggers, search submit highlights, active tab indicators, and critical callouts.
- **Secondary Chrome (`#0f172a` - Deep Slate/Navy):** Forms the foundational command layer across universal headers, primary navigation banners, utility bars, and anchor footers, creating a crisp spatial boundary against product content.
- **Tertiary Utility (`#059669` - Fresh Deal Emerald):** Dedicated strictly to value propositions, savings tags, prime seller verification badges, stock availability status, and promo vouchers.
- **Neutral Foundations:**
  - Base Page Canvas: `#f8fafc` (Soft cool slate)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#ffffff` (Pure white)
  - Sub-surfaces & Inactive Rails: `#f1f5f9` (Subtle boundary slate)
  - Border & Dividers: `#e2e8f0` (Crisp low-noise boundary)
  - Body Text & Primary Headings: `#0f172a` (Max contrast charcoal slate)
  - Secondary Metadata & Ratings Count: `#64748b` (Balanced neutral)

## Typography

The typographic architecture balances high-impact commercial promotional messaging with high-density editorial efficiency:

- **Display & Section Titles (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its geometric warmth and distinct, contemporary letterforms. Used on promo banners, department category headings, product titles on detail pages, and numerical price highlights to ensure prominent eye tracking across dense screens.
- **Body & Product Metadata (`Inter`):** Deployed for high legibility in dense contexts such as specifications, multi-line titles in product tiles, customer reviews, breadcrumb navigation, and filter facet listings.
- **Pricing Notation Rule:** Currency symbols are set at a smaller vertical superscript or label scale relative to the whole-integer price value (e.g., currency symbol using `label-md` baseline-aligned to `price-card` integer, with fractional cents set to `label-md` superscript), enabling instant numerical scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptive 12-column grid system calibrated to support multi-faceted shopping journeys, robust search filtration, and horizontal scrolling product reels:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Max container width capped at 1440px centered, utilizing a 12-column structure with `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px) and `margin: 1.5rem` (24px). Accommodates persistent left-rail facet filtering (3 columns / ~280px) and an adaptive product grid (9 columns / 3 to 4 product tiles per row).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar filters collapse into an off-canvas drawer with an explicit trigger bar. Product cards reflow to a 2 or 3-column matrix.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout utilizing `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem` (12px) and edge `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px). Product listings adapt to 2-column compact tiles or unified horizontal list views.
- **Horizontal Product Rails:** Multi-SKU product carousels (e.g., "Trending Deals", "Recommended For You") use continuous track overflow with peek-edge visibility on mobile/tablet and absolute-positioned arrow pagination pills on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is executed through a disciplined combination of low-contrast ghost boundaries and ambient, micro-soft shadows to preserve card separation without producing visual weight:

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Default card resting state. Crisp 1px solid border (`#e2e8f0`) against canvas `#f8fafc` with no drop shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card Hover / Subtle Lift):** Active hover state for interactive product tiles, category tiles, and department cards. Employs a diffused shadow tinted with slate: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and a border transition to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Popovers):** Mega menu category overlays, user account flyouts, and autocomplete search panels. Features: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Sticky Checkout Bars):** High-priority overlays, quick-view lightboxes, and cart preview slideouts: `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.20)`.
- **Search Header Glass/Scrim:** Sticky header utility employs a 98% opaque dark slate background with a 1px boundary line beneath it (`#1e293b`), creating distinct separation from scrolling product feeds.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an intentional, structured shape scale (`roundedness: 1`) to ensure optimal information density and an uncluttered visual plane:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to form fields, search inputs, rating badges, deal chips, table cells, and thumbnail containers. Ensures clean corners that do not waste internal bounding box space.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to product cards, promotional hero carousels, modal viewports, flyout menus, and department category banners.
- **Pill Utility (Fully Rounded):** Restricted specifically to floating flash-sale timers, verified prime trust tags, cart count notification badges, and floating scroll indicators.

## Components

### 1. Global Navigation Bar & Integrated Search
- **Container:** Dark slate background (`#0f172a`) housing brand mark, deliver-to location pin, integrated multi-part search bar, language/currency selector, account tray, and cart badge.
- **Compound Search Bar:** Integrated white input cluster with three regions:
  1. *Category Scope Dropdown:* Sub-element anchored left with subtle slate boundary (`#f1f5f9`), displaying active department with a chevron.
  2. *Search Input:* Auto-expanding text area with light placeholder text (`#94a3b8`) and clear-all icon.
  3. *Submit Action:* Bold amber/orange square button (`#ea580c`) featuring a centered search icon. On focus within input, the entire composite search receives a 2px outer ring in `#ea580c`.

### 2. Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white surface (`#ffffff`) surrounded by a 1px `#e2e8f0` border.
- **Image Area:** 1:1 square ratio with neutral background (`#ffffff`), centered asset display, and hover-triggered secondary thumbnail fade or gentle scale (1.03x).
- **Badge Anchor:** Absolute top-left positioned chips for "Best Seller" (slate), "Deal of the Day" (emerald `#059669`), or "% Off" (amber-orange `#ea580c`).
- **Product Title:** Two-line truncated string (`body-md`), transitioning to `#ea580c` on hover.
- **Social Proof / Ratings:** Gold star rating row (`#f59e0b`) paired with a numeric count link in `#64748b`.
- **Pricing Stack:** Prominent bold price in `price-card` scale, paired with strikethrough original MSRP in `#94a3b8` and localized savings calculation text.

### 3. Action Buttons & Quick CTAs
- **Primary ("Add to Cart" / "Proceed to Checkout"):** Solid high-visibility amber/orange (`#ea580c`) with white bold text (`label-lg`), 8px border radius, and smooth hover transition to `#c2410c`.
- **Secondary ("Buy Now"):** Solid deep navy/slate (`#0f172a`) with white bold text, signaling immediate direct conversion.
- **Secondary Utility / Quick View:** Crisp 1px outline (`#cbd5e1`) button with white background, slate text, and instant fill to `#f8fafc` on hover.

### 4. Flash Sale Countdown & Urgent Deal Ribbons
- **Flash Sale Banner:** Contrasting slate-black ribbon with an embedded emerald deal badge, countdown clock units displaying hours/minutes/seconds in monospaced high-contrast blocks (`#1e293b` fill, `#ffffff` text), and a live linear stock depletion progress bar (emerald `#059669` track on `#e2e8f0` trough).

### 5. Filter & Facet Sidebar
- **Facet Groups:** Collapsible accordions with minimal chevron toggles, multi-select checkboxes with customized 4px rounded checks (`#ea580c` checked fill), star-rating selector rows, and dual-handle slider for price band selection.

### 6. Badges & Trust Accents
- **Prime / Express Delivery Badge:** Emerald accent (`#059669`) pill or checkmark indicating authenticated fast shipping.
- **Stock Alert:** Micro-label in bold emerald (`In Stock`) or urgent warning terracotta (`Only 3 left in stock - order soon`).
